# Case Study 03 — Structuring / Smurfing (STR Review)

> **Synthetic case — for portfolio demonstration only. All names, accounts, and figures are fictional.**

## Case Reference
`CS-STR-03` (synthetic)

## Typology
**Structuring ("smurfing")** — multiple cash deposits kept just below the Currency Transaction Report (CTR) threshold to avoid reporting triggers.

## Background / Trigger

A bank's AML system generated a **structuring alert** after detecting a pattern of near-threshold cash deposits into a personal current account held by **"Bilal K."** (fictional individual), a declared salaried employee with a stated monthly income inconsistent with the deposit volume observed.

## Investigative Methodology

1. **Transaction pattern analysis** — 90-day account history pulled and deposits plotted by date, amount, and branch location.
2. **KYC/CDD file review** — declared occupation, income bracket, and source-of-funds statement compared against actual account activity.
3. **Threshold-proximity analysis** — deposits assessed for clustering just below the CTR reporting threshold.
4. **Beneficiary/counterparty check** — outbound transfers from the account traced to identify ultimate recipients.
5. **STR drafting** per FMU reporting format, with a clear narrative linking the pattern to the structuring typology.

## Red Flags Identified

- **14 cash deposits over 45 days**, each individually below the CTR reporting threshold, made across **5 different branch locations**.
- Deposits made on **consecutive or near-consecutive business days**, a pattern inconsistent with normal salary/personal cash-handling behavior.
- Declared occupation (salaried employee, mid-level income) **inconsistent** with aggregate deposit volume (~6x stated annual income within the 45-day window).
- Majority of funds **transferred out within 24–48 hours** of deposit to a small number of recipient accounts, rather than being retained or spent through normal account usage.
- No plausible business or asset-sale rationale documented in the customer's KYC file for the cash volume observed.

## Outcome / Recommendation

- **STR filed** with FMU citing structuring/smurfing typology and the mismatch between declared income and account activity.
- Recommended the account be placed under **enhanced monitoring** and outbound recipient accounts be screened for related suspicious activity.
- Recommended a **source-of-funds follow-up request** be issued to the customer as part of ongoing CDD.
